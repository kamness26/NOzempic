Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

NOzempic is a private, personal health coaching tool for one user. This policy explains how health and activity data collected from the Oura Ring and Renpho scale is handled.

Data We Collect

We collect health and fitness data from the Oura Ring, including activity scores, readiness, sleep performance, and heart rate variability. Body composition data may also be collected via Renpho PDF uploads.

How We Use Your Data

Data is used exclusively to generate a weekly private coaching email. No data is shared with any other person or entity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your health data to any third parties. Data is processed by the Anthropic API solely to generate personalized coaching text and is not stored or used for training purposes.

Data Storage

Activity data is fetched in real time each week and is not permanently stored. Body composition data uploaded for processing is handled locally and not retained after weekly processing is complete.

Access & Deletion

To request deletion of any stored data, contact the app administrator directly.
